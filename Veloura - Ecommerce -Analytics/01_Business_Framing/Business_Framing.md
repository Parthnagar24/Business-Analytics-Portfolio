**Project:** #Veloura (Fashion & Beauty Ecommerce, India)  
**Date:** 14 September 2026

# What we did in this phase:  
-------------------------------------------------
We defined the problem we're solving and drew the boundaries of the project before touching any data.

# Business problem:
--------------------------------
#Veloura's leadership has no unified view of how revenue is leaking from three connected areas: high return rates on certain products, promotions that may be losing money after discounts, and high-value customers quietly churning. These three problems are likely related to each other, not separate issues.

# Scope:
-------------
- #Geography: India only
- #Categories: Fashion (clothing, footwear, hats) and Beauty (makeup, soap)
- #Timeline: 2023 (company founded) to 14 September 2026
- #Out of scope: international operations, supplier/inventory root-cause investigation, website UX issues

# **Stakeholders:**  
----------------------
CEO and CFO (final report recipients), Operations Manager (returns), Marketing Manager (promotions), Sales Manager (retention/churn) — reporting through the Senior Analyst.
# Success criteria — 5 key questions the finished project must answer:
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Total revenue leakage (₹) from returns, bad promotions, and churn — combined.
2. Which categories/products drive the most return-related loss.
3. Which promotions are profitable vs. which lose money after discounts.
4. Which high-value customers have churned, and when it started.
5. Whether the three problems are connected (e.g., promoted products returned more, or returns causing churn).
